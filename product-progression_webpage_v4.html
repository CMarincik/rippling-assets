seven patterns
Reading the 29-month arc

What the timeline tells you.

Seven patterns worth knowing when you step back from the individual launches.

i.

Country expansion has a rhythm

UK (Mar 2024), EOR to 80 countries (Mar 2025), Ireland (Aug 2025), Germany (Sep 2025), Inventory Management Europe (May 2026). Roughly six months between country milestones.

ii.

IT graduated into its own product

Mid-2024 IT was Device Trust, Pangolin, and MDM expansion. Mid-2026 it's Remote Access, Access Reviews, Automated Compliance (SOC 2), Attribute Mapping V2, Device Migration tooling, and Device Buyback with a dedicated release cadence.

iii.

Spend caught up and then some

Bill Pay (Sep 2024), Spend Management standalone (Sep 2024), Travel (Aug 2025), Proactive Spend Accounting (Feb 2026), Anomaly Detection (Mar 2026), Metal Cards Alpha (Apr 2026), multi-ERP integrations. The Sep 2024 standalone launch was the inflection point.

iv.

AI shifted from features to product

Talent Signal (Sep 2024) and AI Application Review (Oct 2025) were AI features inside other products. Rippling AI (Mar 2026) is the named, top-level product, with Interview Assistant rolling under it.

v.

Global keeps expanding the floor

EOR went from 40 to 80 countries. Global Contractor Hub gave contractors their own surface. Germany got the full stack. Ireland launched. Inventory Management Europe and DATEV followed. Pattern: expand floor coverage, then layer in country-specific compliance.

vi.

Workflow Studio is the connective tissue

From internal release notes in late 2024 to a public launch in Jan 2025, expanded in May and July, then elevated to "Rippling Platform" positioning in June 2025. It's underneath almost every cross-product feature.

vii.

Rippling now sells engineering and advisory, not just software

Three launches in 12 months point the same direction. Workforce Advisory (Nov 2025) for accountants and HR advisory firms. Rippling Solutions (Feb 2026) with Forward Deployed Engineers building custom apps inside the customer's Rippling. A continuous build-out of Rippling for Accountants tools. The answer to "can Rippling do X?" shifted from "wait for the roadmap" to "we'll build it."
